⚡ GridRunner Setup

🎉 No license key needed. Your bot starts a free 7-day trial automatically. Just leave LICENSE_KEY blank during setup.

What You Need

1 A Solana wallet with some USDC

2 A free Render.com account

3 A free Alchemy.com account
⚠️ Render needs a credit card (even for free tier). They do a $1 hold that gets refunded. You won't be charged.

Step 1 — Get Alchemy Key

1. Go to Alchemy → Create App
2. Select Solana → Mainnet
3. Copy the API Key

Step 2 — Deploy Bot

🚀 Deploy to Render

On the Render page:

• Name: anything you want
• Branch: release
• Plan: Free

Add these 4 things:

FieldWhat to put
SOL_PRIVATE_KEYPhantom → Settings → Export Private Key
SOL_WALLET_ADDRESSYour wallet address (copy from Phantom)
ALCHEMY_KEYThe API key from Step 1
LICENSE_KEYLeave blank — trial starts automatically
• Click "Create Web Service"
• Wait ~2 minutes for deploy
• Your bot URL: https://your-name.onrender.com

Step 3 — Start Trading

1. Open your bot URL → you'll see the GridRunner dashboard
2. Pick Grid + BTC/USDC (or any pair)
3. Leave it in Paper mode first
4. Click Start
5. When ready, tap Paper: ON to switch to LIVE

⏰ Keep Bot Awake

Render's free plan sleeps after 15 min of no visitors:

1. Sign up at UptimeRobot.com (free)
2. Add monitor → HTTP(s) → your bot URL
3. Set to every 5 minutes

Now it stays awake 24/7.

After 7 Days

Trial ends → bot switches to paper-only. Buy a license at gridrunner.tools and add it to LICENSE_KEY on Render.

Troubleshooting

• Swaps fail? Need ~$5 USDC + 0.01 SOL for gas
• Not loading? Wait 30 seconds, Render is booting
• Stuck in paper? Tap the Paper button on dashboard
• Other issues? Check Render → Logs
