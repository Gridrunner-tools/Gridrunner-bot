⚡ GridRunner — Setup Guide

🎉 Free 7-Day Trial: Your bot starts a trial automatically — no license key required. Skip that field during setup and you're good to go. After 7 days, buy a license to keep live trading.

What You Need

  1. A Solana wallet with some USDC (Phantom, Solflare, etc.)
  2. A free Render.com account — sign up here
  3. A free Alchemy.com account — sign up here (Solana chain)
⚠️ Important: Render requires a credit card on file even for the free tier. They do a $1 verification hold that is refunded immediately. You will not be charged unless you upgrade.

Step 1: Get Your Alchemy Key

1. Sign up at alchemy.com (free tier)
2. Create new app → select Solana → Mainnet
3. Copy the API key from your dashboard — you'll paste it into Render in Step 2

Step 2: Deploy to Render

1. Click this button to start the deploy:

🚀 Deploy to Render

2. On the Render setup page, check these settings:

SettingValue
Branchrelease
Blueprint Pathrender.yaml
PlanFree — click "Change" if it shows Starter

If it says "Blueprints require a paid plan":
Skip the blueprint. Instead go to Render dashboard → New Web Service → connect gridrunner-tools/gridrunner-bot → branch release → select Free plan. Then manually add the env vars below.

3. Add these environment variables:

VariableValueWhere to Find It
SOL_PRIVATE_KEYYour private keyPhantom → Settings → Export Private Key
SOL_WALLET_ADDRESSYour wallet addressPhantom — copy from top
ALCHEMY_KEYYour Alchemy keyAlchemy dashboard → API Key
LICENSE_KEYLeave this blankYour 7-day trial starts automatically ✨

4. Click "Create Web Service" — your bot deploys in ~2 minutes
5. Your bot URL will be https://[name].onrender.com — find it on your Render dashboard

Step 3: Start Trading

1. Open your Render URL in a browser → you'll see the GridRunner dashboard
2. Select Grid strategy, pick a pair like SOL/USDC
3. Start in Paper mode first to test safely
4. Click Start — the grid begins running
5. When ready, toggle to LIVE mode

Testing Checklist

☐Paper mode — run grid, confirm trades show in history
☐Manual buy (paper) — try a small manual buy/sell
☐Live $1 buy — switch to LIVE, buy $1 of SOL, verify on Solscan
☐Live sell — sell it back, confirm USDC returns
☐Grid live — let grid run, verify buy→sell cycles

⏰ Keep It Alive (Important!)

Render's free tier puts your bot to sleep after 15 minutes of no visitors.
You need a free ping service to visit your bot every 5 minutes:
  1. Sign up at UptimeRobot.com (free)
  2. Click + Add New Monitor
  3. Type: HTTP(s) — URL: your Render URL (e.g. https://gridrunner-xxxx.onrender.com)
  4. Monitoring interval: Every 5 minutes
  5. Click Create Monitor
Now your bot stays awake 24/7 and never misses a trade.

What Happens After 7 Days?

Your trial lets you trade live for 7 full days. After that:
  • The bot stays running but switches to paper-only mode
  • Your positions remain — you won't lose anything
  • To continue live trading, buy a license at gridrunner.tools
  • Add your license key to the LICENSE_KEY env var on Render and redeploy

Troubleshooting

  • Bot starts in paper? Toggle the paper mode switch on the dashboard
  • Swaps failing? You need ~$5 USDC minimum + 0.01 SOL for gas
  • Dashboard not loading? Wait 30 seconds — Render takes a moment to boot
  • Dashboard shows 404? Your Render URL might still be building — check Render dashboard
  • Other issues? Check Render dashboard → Logs for error messages
